# Scientific algorithms and critical decision rules — FAB Spatial & Yield Intelligence

## Exact spatial evidence methodology

**W1 Data geometry:** identify coordinate system and origin; units, wafer radius, notch/orientation, edge exclusion, reticle origin/die layout, layer/step, sampling mask and missing sites. Separate sparse measurement points, dense metrology, defect point cloud, die-bin categorical map, and continuous fields into different typed data contracts.

**W2 Feature extraction:** derive radial/edge/center, sector, directional gradients, reticle periodicity and local-cluster statistics from *observed* points. Normalize radius from the verified coordinate origin and true physical wafer radius, never the farthest observed sample. Snap only radial fractions within the implementation's relative coordinate-tolerance scale to the exact 0.2/0.5/0.8 region boundaries so mathematically equivalent rotated sensor frames cannot change bins due to floating-point rounding. For a sparse map, show points and computed region statistics with coverage; spatial interpolation is a second separately evaluated feature with its own errors and uncertainty. Avoid routine image CNNs until simple descriptors fail a held-out task.

C01 local-pattern screening keeps the fixed raw observed-value same-radius method for sparse/partial maps. For dense maps only (at least 200 numeric sites, verified orientation and broad quadrant support), the candidate removes a fitted broad linear plane before same-radius residual comparison and adapts cell size to observed density. This resolved a synthetic broad-gradient masking challenge, but can absorb overlapping wafer-scale structure; groups remain exploratory, and no measurement is inferred at empty locations.

**W3 Anomaly scoring:** compare with matching product/recipe/tool/measurement/sampling baselines; account for missingness, sampled regions and repeated wafer measurements. Calibrate false-alert rate on held-out wafers/lots and periods, not neighboring pixels from training wafers. Require multiplicity correction for multiple spatial searches.

P3 C04 uses one leave-one-wafer-out max-T rank decision across measured sites. It requires at least 20 earlier, distinct lots; the minimum empirical p-value is `1/(n+1)`, or 0.0476 at 20 baseline wafers. Before that screen, the engine checks for ordered change in the whole-wafer, radial, sector, and populated radial-sector profiles. It permutes complete wafer profiles so site dependence is retained and returns `INCONCLUSIVE` when the max temporal-shift statistic crosses its declared 0.01 synthetic screening level. This screen can miss unrepresented or weaker spatial shifts. `NO_SHIFT_DETECTED` is not evidence that the baseline is stationary. Sampling masks and product/tool/chamber context must still match; incompatible subgroups are rejected. The max-T alert is conditional on the supplied eligible population, and no site is individually significant.

For repeated target investigations, C04 optionally accepts a bounded caller-declared complete, predeclared target family and uses Bonferroni alpha `0.05 / family_size` across target analyses. The per-wafer max-T remains the internal spatial multiplicity control. If the empirical rank floor `1/(n+1)` cannot resolve the adjusted alpha, C04 abstains instead of reporting a family-level decision. P3 records the caller's family ID, size and selection rule, but cannot audit that the external roster is actually complete or that targets outside it were not searched. Without a family declaration, results remain single-target conditional screens; repeated/adaptive searches are unadjusted. The fixed-baseline synthetic null study observed a false alert in at least one of 20 repeated targets in 9/20 Gaussian families and 11/20 t(3) families with 20 baseline lots; these Monte Carlo results motivate the adjustment but do not establish manufacturing calibration.

**W4 Historical similarity:** select geometric normalization and descriptor distance appropriate to measurement modality. Lock train/holdout splits by wafer/lot and time, evaluate top-k relevance against independent labeled similar-but-not-causal cases, and explain match regions. Never label top nearest neighbors as verified root cause.

**W5 Evolution:** align coordinate systems and verify measurement meaning, product/layer and wafer identity before subtraction; show registration residuals and transformation uncertainty. Unknown orientation or incompatible units prevents evolution claims. Match sparse-to-sparse observed sites or propagate validated interpolation uncertainty. C02 propagates supplied per-site standard uncertainties only conditionally on independent measurement errors across steps and matched sites; it reports cross-step and cross-site covariance as unavailable rather than treating a generic standard uncertainty as repeatability.

**W6 Yield link:** require correct wafer/die/reticle physical correspondence, outcome time ordering, at least 20 independent lots, an explicit reviewed complete eligible-wafer frame with outcome-blind predeclared eligibility, and a complete hypothesis family declared before holdout outcomes. Multiple wafers per lot are permitted; average their within-wafer Fisher-z estimates within lot, then perform inference over lots and omit whole lots for leave-one-lot-out analysis. This prevents wafer pseudoreplication but assumes lots are independent. A source registry assertion is preserved as provenance but is not independently audited by P3. Until a separately validated estimator exists, probability-sampled, incomplete, outcome-informed or unverifiable holdout cohorts make C03 abstain. If multiple hypotheses are analyzed, report per-hypothesis nominal intervals without implying family-wise significance and label selected associations exploratory unless a separately qualified multiplicity method is supplied. Quantify uncertainty and confounding/product mix. A source-reviewed temporal partition may additionally report per-epoch descriptive associations; it must be fixed before holdout outcomes, meet independent-lot support in every stratum, and label nominal intervals as unadjusted. Opposite directions warn that the pooled result is not stable across time; absence of reversal does not certify stability. Lots may not cross the temporal partition. Without authorized epoch metadata, hidden temporal context change is not identifiable. State observational association, not proof an upstream pattern created failure.

**OSS selection:** scikit-image, PySAL/esda, scikit-learn, NumPy and wafer-map visualization implementations may be useful; for first bootstrap a stdlib-only observed-region baseline is included. Validate transitive dependencies, renderer host and actual geometry. Avoid expensive model training without real added value.


## Minimum correct implementation order
A. Validate source/identity/units/permissions BEFORE scientific analysis. B. Implement an independently understandable deterministic baseline and a fixed positive/negative oracle. C. Create an evidence envelope and show a complete user workflow. D. Add the full required scientific methods with assumption diagnostics and falsifying cases. E. Compare advanced OSS only where it can beat or materially extend that baseline; freeze test cases first. F. Run the combined product and real target-OS/offline qualification. G. Repeat audit until all gates pass.

## Mathematical and evaluation requirements
- For numeric estimates, specify the estimand, unit, effective independent sample, uncertainty method, algorithm/version and declared applicability domain.
- For classification, retrieval and anomaly cases, precommit held-out metrics, precision/recall/calibration or appropriately justified equivalents. Never fit thresholds to evaluation answers.
- Use group/time/device/wafer/lot/experiment-aware holdouts where dependencies require them; random measurement-row splits are not acceptable evidence of broader generalization.
- Distinguish data observed, data estimated and data unknown; refuse if physical constraints or study assumptions cannot be verified.
- Run property, metamorphic, adversarial and counterfactual *tests* where meaningful (e.g. changing labels must not create a spurious causal claim; adding an unapproved join must trigger refusal).
- An LLM explanation must be mechanically checked against the verified evidence object. An independent code-based oracle, not an agent's agreement, determines scientific pass/fail.

## Scientific peer review rubric
Engineer/reviewer should be able to answer: Is the population actually eligible? What was the measured unit and its grain? Which approved sources and joins were used? Could sampling or timing explain the observation? Is the statistical estimand identifiable? Which alternative explanations were tested? Which result is observed rather than predicted? What result would invalidate the conclusion? Does the method generalize to unseen wafers/lots/tools/periods? What is explicitly unknown?
